Sunbird, the Android app that does iMessage, returns from the dead with an open beta

06 August 2026·Peter·2 min read

The EU has been throwing its weight around, trying to force interoperability between the major digital platforms. But companies can work at the issue too – companies like Sunbird, which may as well be called “phoenix” since it’s back from the dead.

Sunbird has built an Android app that promises proper iMessage support. Is that still necessary now that Apple devices support RCS? The company points out that RCS messages still appear in a green bubble to iPhone users. Also, some features like reactions, replies and iMessage group features still don’t work properly when RCS gets involved.

So, Sunbird – the app promises full support for iMessage chats without requiring any additional hardware. It also supports RCS and SMS/MMS, so all your texting can reside in one app (there’s more coming soon – read below).

The Sunbird app promises full support for iMessage chats

The Sunbird app can be downloaded from the Google Play Store. Note that this is a paid app – you get a 14-day free trial, after that it’s $2.99 a month or $24.99 a year (this is after a recent price hike from $1.99 a month). The company says that 33% of installs started the free trial (the app was in a closed beta for a time) and 8% converted to paying subscribers.

We should warn you that while the company promises that messages are encrypted on your device and are not stored on Sunbird servers longer than it is necessary to send them to other chat members, the company does not have the best track record when it comes to security.

Back in 2023, Sunbird partnered with Nothing for Nothing Chats. However, not even a week later, the Sunbird-powered Chats app was pulled from the Play Store over privacy concerns and a few days after that the Sunbird service was shut down. It lay dormant since then – or rather the app was reworked and it returned with a closed beta. Now, it’s available to all that want to try it as part of an open beta.

The new Primary and Secondary tabs

The app arrives with a new feature – two tabs that sort messages into Primary and Secondary. People you talk to often get filed under the Primary tab, while one-off messages (e.g. verification codes) and messages from unknown numbers go in the Secondary tab.

The company also has big plans for the future. It wants to add support for WhatsApp and Facebook Messenger chats, so that you can really do all your texting with one app. This is currently in “late-stage development” and is expected to launch in Q3 (i.e. by the end of September).

AI features are in the works too, of course. Sunbird Intelligence will add an on-device chat summary feature that will help catch you up to busy chats. Also, there will be a built-in, on-device assistant that will be able to carry out simple tasks like writing a reply to messages. The AI features are also planned for a Q3 launch.
