Indian iQOO Z11's processor officially confirmed, and it's different from other models

07 August 2026·Sagar·1 min read

Last month, a media report claimed that the iQOO Z11 - launching in India on August 20 - will be powered by the Dimensity 7500 Turbo SoC, making it the first smartphone in India to have that MediaTek chip at the helm. Well, that report turned out to be correct, as iQOO has announced that the Indian Z11 will have the Dimensity 7500 Turbo under the hood.

In comparison, the Chinese and Malaysian Z11 are powered by the Dimensity 8500 and Snapdragon 7s Gen 4, respectively.

That said, in addition to confirming the Indian Z11's chipset, iQOO also gave us our best official look yet at the smartphone, which will come in Aurora Green and Celestial Blue colors. It will be 7.99mm thick and weigh 199g. iQOO previously revealed the Indian Z11 will pack a curved display, unlike its Chinese and Malaysian counterparts, which feature flat screens.
